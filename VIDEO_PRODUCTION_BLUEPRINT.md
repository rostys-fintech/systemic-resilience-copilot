# Hack Apertus — Video Production Blueprint

Product: **Systemic Resilience Copilot**  
Target length: **2:30–2:45**  
Format: **16:9, 1080p, 30 fps, English voice-over, burned-in English captions**  
Primary objective: show a working product and one memorable decision path, not explain the research paper.

## Production principle

The video should feel like a short product film, not a screen recording with commentary.

The judge should understand four things without effort:

1. a backup can exist and still fail to recover the workload in time;
2. Apertus turns messy language into structured evidence and later challenges/explains the result;
3. deterministic rules control the decision and preserve UNKNOWN;
4. fixing one weakness exposes the next evidence gap instead of producing a fake “all clear”.

---

# STAGE 1 — DIRECTORIAL LOCK

## Visual style

- clean institutional / fintech look;
- use the live product as the main visual asset;
- no talking-head video unless needed for the opening/closing;
- no research tables, manuscripts, equations, or long technical trace on screen;
- no decorative stock footage;
- no aggressive cinematic effects;
- use subtle zooms, cursor emphasis, short callout captions, and clean cuts.

## Rhythm

- first 10 seconds: problem immediately;
- every 10–20 seconds something visually changes;
- never leave a static screen while the narration continues for too long;
- pauses should happen on the important results: **95 > 60**, **S5 RED / B2**, **S6 UNKNOWN**, **S7 UNKNOWN**.

## Viewer journey

`Problem → Live scenario → Apertus extraction → deterministic decision → Apertus explanation → intervention → next UNKNOWN → systemic lens → close`

# STAGE 2 — FINAL VOICE-OVER SCRIPT

Target spoken speed: **135–150 words/minute**.  
Target total: **330–370 words**.

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook / problem

**Voice-over**

“Having a backup does not mean a bank can actually recover its critical workload in time. The harder question is: which recovery assumption fails first — and what should we do next?”

**Screen**
- open on product hero;
- subtle zoom to `95 minutes` vs `60 minutes`;
- on-screen callout: **Backup exists ≠ executable recovery**.

---

## 0:15–0:30 — Product

**Voice-over**

“This is Systemic Resilience Copilot. Apertus handles the messy language. Transparent deterministic rules control the recovery decision, so missing evidence stays UNKNOWN instead of becoming a guess.”

**Screen**
- show the six-step product flow;
- briefly highlight `Scenario → Apertus → Rules → Decision`;
- keep `Apertus live` visible.

---

## 0:30–1:00 — Apertus structures the scenario

**Voice-over**

“Our synthetic bank has a retail-payments workload with a sixty-minute tolerance. The recovery route is independent and has been tested, but the observed recovery takes ninety-five minutes. Apertus reads the normal-language scenario and structures only the facts that are actually stated. The host then validates them before any decision is made.”

**Screen actions**
1. click **Structure with Apertus & analyse**;
2. show extraction animation;
3. pause on validated facts;
4. show **KNOWN → UNKNOWN → NEEDED NEXT** for 2–3 seconds.

**Callout**
- `60 min target`
- `95 min observed`
- `shared demand / capacity = UNKNOWN`

---

## 1:00–1:22 — Deterministic decision

**Voice-over**

“The rule chain stops at the first binding condition. Here, recovery independence passes, but execution fails: ninety-five minutes is longer than the sixty-minute tolerance. The first binding state is B2. The correct action is to repair and retest execution — not jump to a capacity solution.”

**Screen**
- pause on **Recovery is too slow**;
- visually emphasize **95 min > 60 min**;
- optionally open technical trace for only 1–2 seconds, then close it.

**Callout**
**S5 RED · B2 → REPAIR EXECUTION**

---

## 1:22–1:42 — Apertus challenge / explanation

**Voice-over**

“Now Apertus challenges and explains the already-fixed decision. It can identify missing evidence and explain why the conclusion follows, but it cannot override the deterministic recovery state.”

**Screen actions**
1. click **Why? Ask Apertus**;
2. show the three-step processing animation;
3. pause on **Apertus live review**;
4. briefly highlight `Missing evidence` and validated references.

**Callout**
**AI explains. Rules decide.**

---

## 1:42–2:02 — Apply the narrow fix

**Voice-over**

“We fix only the execution problem and rerun the same workload. Recovery now completes in forty-five minutes. S5 turns green — but the Copilot does not declare the system resilient. The next unresolved condition becomes visible instead.”

**Screen actions**
1. click **Apply the fix**;
2. show repair / retest / rerun animation;
3. pause on before / after;
4. emphasize `95 → 45` and `S6 UNKNOWN`.

**Callout**
**Fix one weakness → expose the next evidence gap**

---

## 2:02–2:28 — Systemic lens

**Voice-over**

“Only after bank-level recovery passes do we ask the systemic question. In this supporting synthetic case, three institutions may need the same recovery fabric. But assured capacity, priority and coordination evidence are missing. So the correct result is S7 UNKNOWN — measure capacity and obtain entitlement evidence. The tool refuses to invent a shortage.”

**Screen actions**
1. click **Open systemic lens**;
2. show Aurora, Meridian and Harbor entering the shared ResilienceGrid scenario;
3. run systemic evidence animation;
4. pause on **S7 UNKNOWN** and the refused conclusion.

**Callout**
**Shared recovery resource ≠ proven capacity shortage**

---

## 2:28–2:42 — Close

**Voice-over**

“Systemic Resilience Copilot turns messy resilience evidence into a traceable next action. Apertus helps structure and explain. Deterministic rules preserve decision integrity. And when the evidence stops, the inference stops.”

**Screen**
- clean final frame with product name;
- subtitle: **Evidence → Decision → Action**;
- production URL and GitHub repository can appear small at the bottom.

# STAGE 3 — SCREEN-RECORDING PLAN

## Browser preparation

Before recording:

- Chrome/Edge at 100% zoom;
- 1920×1080 desktop resolution if possible;
- hide bookmarks bar;
- close unrelated tabs;
- disable notifications;
- open production root in a fresh tab;
- confirm `Apertus live` before recording;
- run the full flow once as rehearsal;
- then reload and record a clean second run.

## Cursor direction

- move the cursor slowly;
- stop on buttons for about 0.5 sec before clicking;
- do not circle the cursor randomly;
- avoid scrolling while speaking an important sentence;
- if the app scrolls automatically, let it finish before the next narration beat.

## Record in separate clips, not one fragile take

Recommended raw clips:

1. `01_hero.mp4` — hero and flow;
2. `02_parse.mp4` — Apertus extraction;
3. `03_decision.mp4` — S5 RED / B2;
4. `04_apertus_why.mp4` — live explanation;
5. `05_fix.mp4` — repair / before-after;
6. `06_systemic.mp4` — systemic lens;
7. `07_close.mp4` — clean closing frame.

This allows one failed live call to be replaced without rerecording the full video.

---

# STAGE 4 — VOICE PRODUCTION

## Preferred voice

- male;
- confident but not dramatic;
- age impression around 25–35;
- natural international English;
- medium-low pitch;
- slightly slower on technical terms;
- no “AI narrator” theatrical tone.

## Delivery notes

Emphasize:

- **“ninety-five minutes”**;
- **“sixty-minute tolerance”**;
- **“first binding condition”**;
- **“AI explains. Rules decide.”**;
- **“UNKNOWN”**;
- **“the tool refuses to invent a shortage.”**

Pause for about 0.3–0.5 sec after each of those lines.

## Preferred workflow

Record / generate the full voice-over first, then edit screen clips to match the audio. This produces much cleaner pacing than narrating while clicking the site live.

## Audio processing

- remove long breaths / dead air;
- keep natural micro-pauses;
- light noise reduction only;
- normalize final narration around -14 to -16 LUFS integrated;
- avoid strong compression that makes the voice sound radio-like;
- no background music under the most technical 95-vs-60 decision moment.

---

# STAGE 5 — EDITING / MOTION DIRECTION

## Editing rule

Every visual effect must help the judge understand the decision path.

Use:

- clean cuts;
- 3–6% digital zoom on the active part of the interface;
- cursor emphasis only at clicks;
- short 1-line overlays;
- subtle motion when moving between product stages.

Avoid:

- glitch effects;
- rapid transitions;
- large animated titles over the UI;
- music drops;
- excessive kinetic typography;
- showing code unless specifically needed.

## On-screen text overlays

Maximum 6 major overlays in the entire video:

1. **Backup exists ≠ executable recovery**
2. **Apertus structures the evidence**
3. **95 min > 60 min → S5 RED / B2**
4. **AI explains. Rules decide.**
5. **95 → 45 → next state becomes UNKNOWN**
6. **Shared recovery resource ≠ proven shortage**

Keep overlays visible for 2–4 seconds.

---

# STAGE 6 — CAPTIONS AND SOUND

## Captions

- English burned-in captions;
- 1–2 lines maximum;
- about 32–40 characters per line where possible;
- sentence case;
- highlight no more than one key term per caption;
- keep captions above browser controls / bottom-safe margin.

## Music

Optional only.

If used:
- very light ambient / modern technology bed;
- around -28 to -32 LUFS below narration;
- fade down further during the live decision / Apertus result;
- no cinematic trailer music.

Silence with a clean voice-over is preferable to distracting music.

---

# STAGE 7 — QUALITY CONTROL

Run three audits before export.

## Science / claim audit

The video must NOT claim:

- prediction of systemic failure;
- a realised European B3 capacity shortage;
- that shared provider = shared failure;
- that multiple providers = recoverability;
- that Apertus makes the final supervisory decision;
- probability / risk scores;
- that synthetic cases are measured incidence.

## Product-truth audit

The video must visibly prove:

- `Apertus live`;
- live scenario extraction;
- deterministic S5 RED / B2;
- live Apertus review;
- intervention rerun;
- S6 UNKNOWN after the fix;
- systemic lens ending at S7 UNKNOWN.

## Viewer audit

Ask of every 10–15 second block:

- can a judge understand what changed?
- is there one clear visual focal point?
- does the voice-over explain the visible action rather than unrelated background theory?

---

# STAGE 8 — FINAL EXPORT / SUBMISSION PACKAGE

## Master export

- MP4 / H.264;
- 1920×1080;
- 30 fps;
- AAC audio 48 kHz;
- target bitrate around 10–16 Mbps;
- final length ideally **2:30–2:45**;
- no watermarks.

## Create three outputs

1. `Systemic_Resilience_Copilot_Demo_MASTER.mp4`
2. `Systemic_Resilience_Copilot_Demo_CAPTIONS.mp4`
3. `Systemic_Resilience_Copilot_Demo_Subtitles.srt`

The captioned MP4 should be the default hackathon upload unless the platform explicitly supports subtitles reliably.

---

# FINAL RECORDING CHECKLIST

Before the final take:

- production site loads normally;
- Apertus status = live;
- browser cache refreshed;
- default synthetic scenario restored;
- microphone / generated voice checked;
- screen recording resolution correct;
- no private tabs, notifications or credentials visible;
- no HF token anywhere on screen;
- one rehearsal completed;
- raw clips saved separately;
- voice-over timing locked;
- final video watched once with sound and once muted to verify visual clarity.

## Production order

**1. Lock narration → 2. create voice track → 3. record clean product clips → 4. edit to narration → 5. add overlays → 6. captions → 7. QC → 8. export.**
