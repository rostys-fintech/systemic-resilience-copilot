# Systemic Resilience Copilot — Judge-First YouTube Strategy V3

## Core principle

The strongest submission video should feel like a **short premium product film**, not a screen recording with commentary and not a research presentation.

Every scene must prove one capability or one decision transition. YouTube is the public player for Devpost, so optimize for **judge clarity, not clickbait**.

## What the judge must understand without effort

1. A backup can exist and still fail to recover the critical workload in time.
2. Apertus turns normal-language recovery evidence into structured facts.
3. Deterministic rules—not the model—set the recovery state and next action.
4. Missing evidence stays `UNKNOWN`.
5. Fixing one weakness exposes the next unresolved condition instead of producing a fake all-clear.
6. The systemic lens refuses to infer a capacity shortage from shared recovery exposure alone.

## Narrative spine

**Problem → Apertus extraction → deterministic decision → Apertus explanation → narrow fix → S6 UNKNOWN → systemic lens → S7 UNKNOWN → close**

Canonical proof path:

**95 min > 60 min → S5 RED / B2 → REPAIR_EXECUTION → 45 min → S6 UNKNOWN → S7 UNKNOWN**

## Directing rules

- Hook the problem in the first 10 seconds.
- Use the live product as the main visual asset.
- Change the visual focus every 10–20 seconds.
- Pause on `95 > 60`, `S5 RED / B2`, `S6 UNKNOWN`, `S7 UNKNOWN`.
- Voice explains the visible action; it does not lecture about research background.
- Use only clean cuts, match cuts on clicks, and a short dissolve at opening/closing.
- No talking head, stock footage, equations, code tours, research tables, glitch effects, preset transition packs, or generic “AI magic” language.

## Voice principle

- confident male voice;
- natural international English;
- calm and precise;
- average editorial pace about **120–130 wpm**;
- slower around proof states;
- voice first, then edit the product footage to the real waveform.

## Major overlays — exactly five maximum

1. **Backup exists ≠ executable recovery**
2. **95 min > 60 min → S5 RED / B2**
3. **AI explains. Rules decide.**
4. **95 → 45 → S6 UNKNOWN**
5. **Shared recovery resource ≠ proven shortage**

The final `Evidence → Decision → Action` line is an end-card line, not a major overlay.

## Final acceptance rule

A judge watching only the video should be able to answer:
- What problem does the product solve?
- What does Apertus actually do?
- What do the deterministic rules do?
- Why is `UNKNOWN` useful?
- What changes after the intervention?
- Why does the systemic lens end at `S7 UNKNOWN`?

If any answer is unclear, the edit is not finished.
