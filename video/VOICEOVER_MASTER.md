## 0:00–0:15 — Hook

Having a backup does not mean a bank can recover a critical workload when it actually matters. The harder question is simple: which recovery assumption fails first, and what action is justified next?

## 0:15–0:31 — Product

This is Systemic Resilience Copilot. Apertus turns messy resilience language into structured evidence. A transparent rule layer then controls the decision, so missing evidence stays UNKNOWN instead of being converted into confidence.

## 0:31–1:02 — Live scenario + Apertus extraction

In this synthetic case, a retail-payments workload has a sixty-minute recovery tolerance. The alternate route is outside the primary failure domain, supports the same workload, and has been tested. But observed recovery takes ninety-five minutes. Apertus extracts only what is stated; the host validates the structure before the rules run. We know the workload, target, path and test result. Shared-demand and capacity evidence remain unknown.

## 1:02–1:25 — Deterministic decision

The rule chain stops at the first binding condition. Recovery independence passes. Execution does not. Ninety-five minutes is longer than sixty. So the first binding state is B two, at S five. The justified action is narrow: repair execution and retest. We do not jump to capacity.

## 1:25–1:45 — Apertus review

Now Apertus reviews the fixed decision. It explains why the evidence supports it, challenges the interpretation, and surfaces what is still missing. But it cannot overwrite the deterministic state. AI explains. Rules decide.

## 1:45–2:05 — Fix and rerun

We repair only the execution problem and rerun the workload. Recovery now completes in forty-five minutes. S five turns green. But the system does not declare resilience. Instead, the next unresolved condition appears: S six, simultaneous recovery demand.

## 2:05–2:31 — Systemic lens

Only now do we open the systemic lens. Three synthetic institutions may need the same recovery fabric. Recovery independence and execution pass, but assured capacity, priority and coordination are not established. The correct result is S seven UNKNOWN. Measure capacity and obtain entitlement evidence. The tool refuses to invent a shortage from shared exposure alone.

## 2:31–2:43 — Close

That is the point of Systemic Resilience Copilot: turn messy evidence into a traceable next action. Apertus structures and explains. Deterministic rules preserve decision integrity. And when the evidence stops, the inference stops.
