## Performance rule

The voice must sound like a person demonstrating a working product, not reading a research abstract. Keep sentences conversational and precise. Slow down around the proof states and leave short pauses so the viewer can read the interface.

Pronunciation:
- Apertus → `uh-PER-tus`
- S5 / S6 / S7 → `S five`, `S six`, `S seven`
- B2 → `B two`
- UNKNOWN → normal word, slightly stressed

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

Most resilience plans can tell you a backup exists. They cannot tell you whether the critical workload will actually recover in time.

## 0:10–0:24 — Product

This is Systemic Resilience Copilot. Apertus turns a natural-language recovery scenario into structured evidence. Deterministic rules then identify the first condition that is actually justified by that evidence.

## 0:24–0:51 — Scenario + Apertus

In this synthetic case, Northstar Bank has a sixty-minute recovery tolerance. The alternate route is independent, supports the same workload, and has been tested. But observed recovery takes ninety-five minutes. Apertus extracts the stated facts and leaves what is missing unresolved. The rules receive only validated evidence.

## 0:51–1:12 — Deterministic decision

Now the decision is simple and traceable. Recovery independence passes, but execution fails. Ninety-five minutes is longer than sixty, so the workflow stops at S five red, B two. The next action is not a broad risk score. It is specific: repair execution and retest.

## 1:12–1:29 — Apertus explanation

Apertus can now challenge and explain that fixed decision. It shows why the conclusion follows and what evidence is still missing, but it cannot override the rule state. AI explains. Rules decide.

## 1:29–1:50 — Narrow fix

We apply only the justified fix. Recovery now completes in forty-five minutes. S five passes. But the system does not declare the bank resilient. Instead, it reveals the next unresolved question: simultaneous recovery demand.

## 1:50–2:13 — Systemic lens

Only then do we open the systemic lens. Three synthetic institutions may rely on the same recovery fabric. Upstream recovery passes, but assured capacity, priority, and coordination are still unknown. So the correct result is S seven UNKNOWN. The next step is to measure capacity and verify entitlements — not invent a shortage.

## 2:13–2:24 — Close

That is the product: evidence becomes a decision, the decision becomes an action, and missing evidence remains visible. When the evidence stops, the inference stops.
